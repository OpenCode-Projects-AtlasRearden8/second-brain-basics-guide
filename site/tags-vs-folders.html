Tags vs Folders: An Honest Comparison

Choose one for organizing and the other for describing. That is the whole
answer; the rest is showing the work.

You will hear strong opinions here: "folders are dead," "tags are chaos,"
and so on. Both camps are half right. Here is the honest version.

What folders are good at

Folders are for one true home per file. Strengths:

  • Physical, visual, drill-down navigation. 20-archive/2026/ reads like

an address. No thinking required to browse.

  • Easy to move. Dragging files between systems is easy, and the

archive ordering (by year) survives any tool.

  • Zero discipline required. A file is in one place; there is nothing

to decide on top of where it lives.

  • No vocabulary drift. No "evergreen" vs "permanent" vs "zettel"

splitting one concept into three tags over the years.

What folders are bad at

A file can only sit in one folder. "Multidisciplinary" notes (say, a note on
scheduling that is relevant to both work and home) can only live at one
address. Folders express one dimension; life has more.

What tags are good at

Tags are descriptions, like keywords on a book spine, not locations:

  • Many labels, one file. #healthcare #automation #draft all on one

note. Nothing gets filed "wrong."

  • Flexible views. Sum #idea across three years without moving a file.
  • Fast to add later. New facet of meaning = new tag; no re-filing.

What tags are bad at

  • They rot silently. Nobody notices you now have machine-learning,

ml, and ai-agents as three near-duplicates — until everything looks
messy.

  • They need pruning (see the "tag hygiene" section of the

<a href="monthly-tidy.html">monthly tidy</a>... in spirit — the tidy is where vocabulary drift
gets caught).

  • They tempt you to organize by tags alone, which mixes two jobs and

does neither: you end up with 200 tags and a home for nothing.

The recommended split

JobTool
Where a file livesFolders (the four corners)
What a file is aboutTags, used very sparingly
Where a file connects[[wiki-links]]

Practical tag hygiene (this is the whole ceremony):

  1. Prefer one broad tag per file, never a cloud. #idea, #ref,

#howto, #person, #healthcare-education — a short fixed menu you do
not grow casually.

  1. The wiki-link is your real connector. It points at a note, not a

word — see <a href="linking-ideas.html">linking ideas</a>.

  1. If you cannot decide a file's home folder, put it in the inbox.

Indecision has a room; it does not refuse entry.

A real example

A note about handover emails between nursing shifts:

  • Folder (home): 10-projects/2026-11-shift-handover-tool/notes.md
  • Tags: none needed, or #idea
  • Wiki-links: [[healthcare-education]], [[agent-skills]]

The folder says where it lives. The links say what it connects. You never
needed sixteen tags, and future-you can find it by browsing the project,
searching "handover," or following the links. Three doors, all open.

Do this now (1 minute)

Write your tag menu in the front of your daily note under Tag menu: — the
five or six broad tags you will allow yourself. Done. You have now out-
tagged most people, permanently.
