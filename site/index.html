Second Brain Basics

A manual for a personal knowledge base made of plain files — no fancy apps required, nothing to subscribe to, nothing that locks you in. Written for busy people, in plain friendly English, one small step at a time.

Read it in order:

  1. Start Here
  2. The Four Corners
  3. Naming That Survives Years
  4. Tags vs Folders: An Honest Comparison
  5. Free Editors: Where to Write
  6. Linking Ideas: The Little Web
  7. Optional: Agent Help from Your Notes
  8. The Monthly Fifteen-Minute Tidy
  9. Glossary

Every chapter stands alone and ends with a tiny “do this now” step, each one under five minutes. The starter vault template lives in starter/ — copy it, rename it, open any editor, and you are running.

Run locally: this whole site is pre-built, and you can rebuild it any time with python3 build.py.
