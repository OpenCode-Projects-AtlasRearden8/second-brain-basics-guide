# idea-one-paragraph-a-day

An Inbox example note — a raw thought, written in one breath, no polish.

Write one paragraph a day for the book instead of aiming at chapters.
Chapters feel big; paragraphs fit a lunch break. After a week there is
material, after a month there is a chapter's worth — and starting is
always the expensive part, so make it tiny.
